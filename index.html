    Getting survey data out of SPSS format

    Pew releases its datasets as SPSS .sav files, which a browser cannot read. Converting one to CSV takes a few lines of Python. docs/CONVERTING-SAV.md in this repository has the script, including how to keep the value labels rather than the numeric codes.

    Codebook

    Every variable in the file, with its response categories and how often each one appears. Set the survey weight here, and mark any codes that stand for refusals or non-responses so they are excluded from percentage bases rather than counted as answers.

    One item

    The distribution of a single question, weighted and unweighted side by side. The gap between the two columns is what the survey weights are correcting for.

    Who answers how

    One question, broken out across every background variable at once. All the panels share a single scale and a line marking the whole-sample figure, so the groups that sit away from the average are the ones that stand out.

    Break out by

    Anything with a manageable number of categories can go here. The starting selection is the background variables found in the file.

    Crosstab

    One question broken out by a grouping variable, as weighted row percentages. The test below the table asks whether the differences between groups are larger than sampling error would produce.

    General support vs. local support

    Two questions answered by the same people: whether they support a technology in general, and whether they support it where they live. The difference between those two percentages is the quantity at the center of the NIMBY literature. Because both answers come from the same respondents, the two percentages are correlated, and the comparison uses McNemar's test on the people who answered the two questions differently.

    Regression

    A crosstab shows one difference at a time. Rural residents are less supportive of a local project, and rural areas also lean Republican, so a rural difference in a crosstab may be a partisan difference wearing rural clothing. A regression estimates each predictor's contribution while the others are held constant, which is what separates the two.

    Predictors

    Check each variable to include. A categorical predictor is entered as one indicator per category except the reference, and its coefficients are differences from that reference. Cases missing on any included predictor are dropped from the model entirely, so adding a predictor with many refusals shrinks the sample.
